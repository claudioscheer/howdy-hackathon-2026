Five ways to say it with type

Left side of login stays. Right side drops the Adaptive Engine card, the soundwave, and the telemetry tiles. The panel becomes a poster: Help your engineers succeed, plus the three supporting lines, set as type that actually occupies the black field.

Keep

Help your engineers succeed. Prepare your team with real interview pressure. Adaptive follow-up. Transcript-grounded coaching.

Kill

The Howdy Adaptive Engine card, live probing chip, soundwave bars, and the four status tiles. They turn a title wall into a dashboard.

Move

Size, rotation, outline, and overflow. Some words should be architecture. Others should recede to a caption.
